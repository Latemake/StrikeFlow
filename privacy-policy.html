FightWise Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-07

FightWise is a training timer app. We designed it to work without creating an account and without collecting personal identity information.

1. Data We Collect

FightWise does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

2. Local Device Storage

FightWise stores settings on your device (for example timer duration, selected language, sound preferences, favorite workouts, and tutorial state) so your preferences remain between sessions.

This data stays on your device and is not sent to our servers.

3. Internet and Analytics

FightWise does not use third-party analytics SDKs or advertising trackers in this version.

4. Audio, Vibration, and Wake Lock

The app can play sounds, vibrate, and keep the screen awake during sessions. These features are used only to support workout timing and can be controlled from app settings.

Training and Premium Features

FightWise is a fitness timer and workout guidance tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.

Premium features are not available for purchase in this version. Any future purchases will be handled through Google Play Billing; FightWise does not collect or store payment card information.

5. Children

FightWise is a general fitness tool and is not directed specifically to children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If changed, we will update the "Last updated" date on this page.

7. Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact latefightwise@gmail.com.
