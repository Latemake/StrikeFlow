## Final symbol generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Final production logo, NOT a mockup. Square 1024x1024 composition on a perfectly solid pure black #000000 background. A minimal clean contemporary FW monogram enclosed by a thick circular timer ring with a small gap at exactly 12 o'clock. F bright electric green #20FF50. W pure white. Circle exactly the same green. Custom geometric sports lettering, strong readable FW, optically balanced. Symbol centered with 8% black margin, no clipping. Extremely clean smooth mathematical shapes, two solid opaque colors only, NO glowing effect, no texture, no particles, no noise, no gradients, no distressed edges, no shadows. This is a professional flat vector-style brand mark for FightWise combat sports training. Do not add a glove, fist, extra tick marks, other text, badges, bolts, or yellow color. The F and W must be distinctly readable, substantial dark separation between monogram and outer ring. Brand mark only.

## Final wordmark generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create a final usable HORIZONTAL WORDMARK for the sports training app FightWise. Exact text verbatim: "FightWise", uppercase F and W, lowercase other letters, correct spelling F i g h t W i s e. The entire composition is only this single wordmark, tightly framed in a wide horizontal 4:1 image on a completely solid pure black #000000 background, with very small even margins around the lettering. Professional custom extra-bold slightly forward-leaning geometric sans serif letterforms. Distinctive yet extremely legible; athletic restraint, smooth precise clean contours, intentional balanced kerning, keep counters clear. 'Fight' colored solid electric neon green #20FF50, 'Wise' solid pure white #FFFFFF, no yellow in the green whatsoever. Clean white and electric green flat fills only, no gradient, NO glow, no shadows, no outline, no underlining, no speed streaks, no grit, no texture, no mist, no icon, no ring, no illustration, no slogan, no presentation board. Wordmark must occupy almost the full width and height of the wide composition with modest safe margins. It should look like a refined modern real-world boxing brand identity, not a gaming or esports logo.

Validation: visually reviewed at mobile menu size; 85 application tests passed. Local preview only; no web or Google Play deployment in this change.
